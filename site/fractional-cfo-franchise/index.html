Frequently asked

Common questions.

What does a fractional CFO do for a franchise brand or franchisee?

For a franchisor, it means system-wide reporting, unit economics, and the financial discipline needed to support new territory sales and lender relationships. For a franchisee, it means cash visibility, margin by location, and a close process that holds up to the franchisor's and any lender's scrutiny.

Does Strategic Finance have franchise experience?

Yes. Juiced Fuel, a multi-unit franchise brand, is a current engagement. Strategic Finance builds the finance function, forecasting, and reporting that a growing franchise system and its franchisees need as the brand expands into new territories.

Can bookkeeping be included for a franchisee?

Yes, where a franchisee operates under a parent CFO engagement, bookkeeping support can be scoped in as part of that relationship. It is never offered as a standalone service; it is always part of a broader CFO engagement, priced to the territory's size and stage.

How does a franchise's cash picture differ from a typical small business?

Franchise cash flow has to account for royalty and marketing fund obligations, territory-level unit economics, and often seasonal or capital-intensive operating models. Strategic Finance builds 13-week cash views and driver-based forecasts that reflect those obligations.
