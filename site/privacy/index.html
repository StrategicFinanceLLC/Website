Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Strategic Finance LLC ("Strategic Finance," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you visit strategicfinance.llc, email us, call us, or exchange text messages (SMS) with us. CFO Growth Experts is a brand of Strategic Finance LLC.

Our QuickBooks Online application, Strategic Finance Connect, has its own policy that covers the accounting data it accesses. See the Strategic Finance Connect Privacy Policy for app-specific data. If the two policies conflict on accounting data accessed through the app, that policy controls.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you give us directly and a small amount of technical information collected automatically:

  • Contact information: your name, email address, phone number (including a mobile number), and business name, when you email us, call us, text us, or become a client.
  • Communications: the content of emails, calls, and text messages you exchange with us, and our replies.
  • Business information: details you share about your company so we can answer your questions or provide the services you engage us for.
  • SMS consent records: your opt-in to receive text messages from us, including the date, the number, and how you opted in, and any later opt-out.
  • Technical data: when you visit the website, our hosting provider records standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested. The website does not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, or tracking pixels.

The website itself has no forms. We do not collect information through the website beyond those server logs.

2. How we use information

  • to respond to your questions and requests, by email, phone, or text message;
  • to schedule appointments and send appointment and service updates;
  • to provide the services you have engaged us for, and to bill for them;
  • to operate, secure, and maintain the website and our systems; and
  • to meet legal, tax, and professional obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. Text messages (SMS)

If you give us your mobile number and agree to receive text messages from Strategic Finance LLC, we use that number to send customer care messages: replies to your questions, and appointment and service updates. We do not send marketing text messages. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Mobile numbers and SMS opt-in and consent data are never shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties or affiliates, under any circumstances. Your mobile information is used only to communicate with you about your questions and the services you have engaged us for.

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message from us. You will receive one message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and no further messages after that. Reply HELP for help, or contact us at support@strategicfinance.llc or 843-886-1705. See the SMS Terms in our Terms of Use for the full program terms.

4. How we share information

We share information only as follows:

  • Service providers that operate our practice on our behalf, such as our email and productivity provider, our customer communications and messaging platform, and our website host. They may use the information only to provide their services to us, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal and safety: where required by law or legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Strategic Finance LLC, our clients, or others.
  • Business transfer: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to confidentiality protections and this Policy.

All of the categories above exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent. That information will not be shared with any third parties, and mobile numbers are never shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

5. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure, including encryption in transit for the website and access limited to Strategic Finance LLC's secured systems. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Retention

We keep information as long as we need it for the purposes in this Policy, including keeping business records and meeting legal, tax, and professional obligations. Client records are handled as described in your engagement agreement and, for data accessed through our QuickBooks Online application, the Strategic Finance Connect Privacy Policy.

7. Your choices

  • Text messages: reply STOP to any message to opt out, or HELP for help.
  • Email: ask us to stop emailing you, and we will, except for messages needed to provide services you have engaged us for.
  • Access, correction, or deletion: email support@strategicfinance.llc. We will respond within 30 days, subject to records we must keep for legal, tax, or professional reasons.

8. Children

Our website and services are intended for business owners and professionals age 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated effective date on this page.
