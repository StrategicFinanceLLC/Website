Strategic Finance provides fractional CFO support to healthcare practices and medical device companies.

Strategic Finance LLC, led by founder Tom Schipper, works as the ongoing CFO for multi-location healthcare practices and medical device companies that need senior financial leadership without a full-time hire. Current and recent engagements include a multi-location healthcare practice and a medical device company commercializing an FDA-cleared implant. The work covers accounting cleanup, driver-based forecasting, cash visibility, and the reporting lenders and investors rely on.

Why this fits healthcare & medical device

Built for regulated, multi-location, and capital-intensive businesses.

  • Multi-location healthcare practices.

    Accounting cleanup, forecasting, budgeting, and cash visibility for practices running multiple locations and insurance-driven revenue. Strategic Finance has done this work for Trusted Results Therapy Group, a multi-location mental health therapy practice in South Carolina.

  • Medical device commercialization.

    Cash runway, margin by product line, and investor-ready financial models for device companies moving from FDA clearance into national commercialization. Amplio Spine, which commercializes an FDA-cleared expandable interlaminar spine implant, is a current engagement.

  • Dental & practice finance.

    Cash-flow consulting for dental practices and finance training delivered through Partners in Practice, built on the same close-process and forecasting discipline used across every engagement.

  • Lender & investor readiness.

    Reporting packages, data rooms, and covenant tracking for practices and device companies preparing for financing, refinancing, or a capital raise.

Frequently asked

Common questions.

Does a fractional CFO for a healthcare practice need industry experience?

It helps. Tom has done accounting cleanup and forecasting, budgeting, and cash visibility work for a multi-location healthcare practice, plus cash-flow consulting for dental practices, so the engagement does not start with a learning curve on how multi-location, insurance-driven revenue works.

What financial problems come up most for medical device companies?

Cash runway against regulatory and commercialization timelines, gross margin by product line, and investor- or lender-ready reporting during fundraising or diligence. Strategic Finance builds driver-based forecasts and reporting packages that hold up under that scrutiny.

Is sensitive healthcare data handled securely?

Engagements run under confidentiality terms, with nondisclosure agreements signed as a matter of course. Sensitive information, including in healthcare engagements, is accessed only as the work requires, and all financial data stays inside the client's own QuickBooks Online rather than a third-party platform.

Is bookkeeping offered on its own for these clients?

No. Bookkeeping is part of a CFO engagement, not a standalone service. Cleanup of the underlying books is built into the work where it's needed, so the forecasts and reporting built on top of it hold up.
