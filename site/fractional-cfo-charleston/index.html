Frequently asked

Common questions about a Charleston fractional CFO.

What does a fractional CFO in Charleston actually do?

A fractional CFO gives an owner-led or investor-backed company senior financial leadership without a full-time hire: driver-based forecasts, cash and runway planning, a close process and KPI reporting, and the reporting lenders and investors ask for. It is ongoing financial leadership, not a one-time project.

Is this remote or local to Charleston?

Strategic Finance is based in Charleston, SC, and works with clients in person and remotely across the Southeast. Tom is a Charleston-based fractional CFO who has worked in-market with manufacturing, healthcare, SaaS, and medical device companies in the region.

How is this different from a bookkeeper or accountant?

A bookkeeper records transactions. Strategic Finance handles the full stack: it builds or cleans up the bookkeeping foundation inside the client's own QuickBooks, then uses that clean data for forecasting, cash planning, and the strategic decisions a CFO is responsible for. Cleanup is part of the engagement, never a separate line item.

What size company does Strategic Finance work with?

Owner-led and growth-stage companies that have outgrown basic bookkeeping but are not ready for a full-time CFO hire. Current and recent engagements span healthcare, medical device, SaaS, and franchise brands based in and around Charleston.
